Features

Everything your chats need on a Mac

The WhatsApp you already use, in a proper Mac app with no limits on what you can save.

Every file downloads

PDFs, Word, Excel, ZIPs, photos and voice notes. Nothing is blocked, and each file lands in Downloads with its real name.

Reply from notifications

New messages appear in Notification Center, grouped by chat. Type a reply right in the banner, or click to jump to the conversation.

All your accounts

Keep personal and business numbers open side by side, each in its own window with its own login. The Dock badge counts them all.

Translate on your Mac

Read guests' messages in your language, see translated notifications, and reply in theirs with the 🌐 button. All on-device: nothing is sent anywhere.

Attach anything

Drag files into a chat or pick them from Finder. Record voice notes with your Mac's microphone.

Private by design

No accounts, no tracking, no servers of ours. SumiChat talks only to WhatsApp Web, and the source code is public.

Stays signed in

Scan the QR code once. Close the window and SumiChat keeps running in the Dock, so you never miss a message.

Mac shortcuts

⌘R reloads, ⌘= and ⌘- zoom, ⌥⌘L opens Downloads, and full screen works like any Mac app.

Updates itself

When there's a new version, one click downloads and installs it, checked against our signature. You stay signed in.

Install

Up and running in a minute

SumiChat is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any app from the App Store. No security warnings to click through.

Download

SumiChat.dmg runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs with macOS 13 or later.

Drag to Applications

Open the disk image and drag SumiChat onto the Applications folder. Prefer a guided setup? Use the installer.

Link your phone

Open SumiChat and scan the QR code. On your phone, go to WhatsApp → Settings → Linked devices → Link a device.

Allow notifications

Click Allow when macOS asks, so new messages show up even when SumiChat is in the background.

Questions

Good to know

Is SumiChat made by WhatsApp?

No. SumiChat is an independent app. It shows the official WhatsApp Web inside a Mac window and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp or Meta.

Is it safe?

Your messages stay end-to-end encrypted exactly as they are on web.whatsapp.com, because that's what SumiChat loads. The app has no servers and collects nothing. It's signed and notarized by Apple, and anyone can read the source code on GitHub.

Does it work with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. Any WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business account that can link a device works. Choose File → New Account… to add another number in its own window (macOS 14 or later).

How does replying from a notification work?

Hover over a message notification and click Reply. SumiChat opens that chat in the background, checks it's the right one, and sends your message. If it can't be sure, it sends nothing, copies your text and tells you, so you can paste it yourself.

How does translation work?

SumiChat uses the translation built into macOS 26, on your Mac. Messages are never sent to a translation service. Click Translate → Translate This Chat to see translations under messages, or the 🌐 next to + to turn what you typed into the chat's language; you check it before it goes into the message box. The first time a language comes up, choose Download for it once.

Can I make calls?

SumiChat shows WhatsApp Web, so calling works only as far as WhatsApp Web supports it. For full voice and video calling, use WhatsApp on your phone or the official app.

Where do downloaded files go?

To your Downloads folder, with their original names. If a file with the same name is already there, the new one gets a number, like Quote (1).pdf. Click the "Saved to Downloads" notification to see the file in Finder.

How do I update?

SumiChat checks once a day. When a new version is out, click Install Update and it updates and reopens by itself. You can also choose SumiChat → Check for Updates… at any time.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit SumiChat and drag it from Applications to the Trash. To also remove its saved login, delete ~/Library/WebKit/com.sumi.chat and ~/Library/Caches/com.sumi.chat. Unlink it from your phone under Linked devices.
