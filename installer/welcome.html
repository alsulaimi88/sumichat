Welcome to SumiChat

SumiChat shows WhatsApp in its own Mac window, and lets you download any file people send you, including PDFs.

  • Files you download are saved to your Downloads folder.
  • New messages show up as Mac notifications.
  • The Dock icon shows how many unread messages you have.

This installer puts SumiChat in your Applications folder. If an older version is open, it will be closed and replaced.
